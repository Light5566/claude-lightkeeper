# Content

## What it is

Shelves, books, chapters and pages: the hierarchy readers browse, with revisions, templates, sorting and the recycle bin.

## Modules

- [[Bookshelves]] — Shelves group books
- [[Books]] — A book holds chapters and pages in order
- [[Chapters]] — A chapter groups pages inside a book
- [[Pages]] — A page is the document people read and edit, in the WYSIWYG or markdown editor
- [[Page Revisions]] — Every page save keeps the previous content as a revision, and drafts are revisions too
- [[Page Templates]] — A page marked as a template can be inserted into new pages, and a book or chapter can name a default template for pages created inside it
- [[Recycle Bin]] — Deleted shelves, books, chapters and pages go to the recycle bin first, where they can be restored or destroyed for good
- [[Sort Rules]] — Sort rules are reusable orderings (by name, date, type) that a book can apply automatically to its chapters and pages
