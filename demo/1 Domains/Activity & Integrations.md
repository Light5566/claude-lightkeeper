# Activity & Integrations

## What it is

What happened and who should hear about it: activity, comments, tags, favourites, watching, webhooks.

## Modules

- [[Tags]] — Name-value tags on any entity, browsable and searchable
- [[Comments]] — Threaded comments on pages, with mentions
- [[Favourites]] — Users can star any entity to find it again from their favourites list
- [[Watching & Notifications]] — Users watch a shelf, book, chapter or page at a chosen level and get emailed about updates and comments
- [[Activity Log]] — One row per action anything in the system takes
- [[Webhooks]] — Outgoing HTTP calls for chosen activity types, sent from a queued job
