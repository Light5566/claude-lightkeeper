# Media

## What it is

Images and file attachments stored alongside content.

## Modules

- [[Images]] — Uploaded images for pages, galleries, covers, avatars and diagrams, resized on demand and stored on a configurable disk
- [[Attachments]] — Files or links attached to a page, served with the page's permissions
