# Access & Permissions

## What it is

Who can log in and what they may see: logins, MFA, single sign-on, invites, roles and per-entity permissions.

## Modules

- [[Entity Permissions]] — Per-shelf, book, chapter or page overrides of what each role may view, create, update or delete
- [[Roles]] — A role bundles system permissions (manage settings, manage users) with default content permissions
- [[Authentication]] — Standard email-and-password login, registration, email confirmation and password reset
- [[Multi-factor Authentication]] — Time-based one-time codes and backup codes as a second factor, which a role can require
- [[Single Sign-on]] — External identity: SAML2, OpenID Connect, LDAP and social providers
- [[User Invites]] — An admin can create a user without a password; the user receives an invite link to set one
